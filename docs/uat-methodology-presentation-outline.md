## Slide 4 — 15-Page UAT KPI Reporting Framework: Four Key Management Views

1. **Overall Test Status:** Pass/Fail/Executing status trends with changing scope and planned cumulative execution. Plan, actual and scope remain distinct.
2. **Workstream Failure Rates:** Failed / Total test cases × 100 for the workstream; retain this denominator rather than failed/executed.
3. **Defect Evolution:** unresolved trends, creation/closure, all/closed and open/resolved by Critical/High/Medium/Low priority. Keep defect progress separate from execution.
4. **Workstream Execution:** stacked status comparison plus reconciliation: Workstream, Planned, Executed, Executed − Planned, To Do, Pass, Fail, Executing, Blocked and Total.

Use four compact descriptive cards. Do not invent numerical values, the exact original appearance or the other eleven report pages. The Decision Centre is a separate synthetic demonstration with documented counting rules, not a reproduction of client reports.
