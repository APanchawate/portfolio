# AI-Assisted Test Management — Knowledge Access and Operational Guidance

## Business challenge
Large, international testing programmes produce extensive documentation, processes and recurring questions. Testers, new joiners and management stakeholders need fast access to reliable guidance without bypassing accountable decision-makers.

## My role
Organized knowledge sources, configured instructions and prompts, tested responses and refined **two internal AI knowledge assistants** on an existing enterprise AI platform.

## Solutions
### Programme Knowledge Assistant
Helped users find relevant programme information, processes and working guidance from approved knowledge sources.

### Test Management Knowledge Assistant
Supported questions about testing methodology, governance, execution processes and operational guidance.

## Business value
- Easier discovery of relevant programme and testing knowledge.
- More consistent guidance for onboarding and recurring operational questions.
- Reduced need to search manually through scattered documentation.
- Support for management and team members without replacing human review or sign-off.

## Responsible use
The assistants supported information retrieval and guidance. Product decisions, release approvals, defect ownership and formal test sign-off remained with the appropriate stakeholders.

## Tools and methods
Enterprise AI platform · Knowledge-source organization · Prompt and instruction design · Testing and refinement · Test-management process knowledge

> Publication note: These were configured assistants on an existing enterprise platform, not a claim to have trained or built a foundation model. No unverified productivity percentage is claimed.
